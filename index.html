Riding a bike be it for leisure, exercise or as a means of transport to work is one of the most effective ways of improving one's welbeing. Riding is an excellent and low-impact exercise offering a wide range of physical, mental and practical benefits that include:

Physical Health Benefits

  • Cardiovascular Strength:

    Regular cycling stimulates your heart and lungs, significantly reducing the risk of heart disease and stroke. Research suggests that cycling to work can cut the risk of developing heart disease by nearly half.

  • Low-Impact on Joints.

    Unlike running, cycling is a non-weight-bearing activity, placing minimal stress on your knees and hips. This makes it an ideal exercise for people with arthritis or those recovering from leg injuries.

  • Weight Loss and Muscle Building:

    Cycling increases your metabolic rate and builds muscle, helping you burn between 400 and 1,000 calories per hour depending on intensity.

  • Disease Prevention:

    It primarily targets the lower body, including the quadriceps, hamstrings, calves, and glutes, while your core and upper body work to maintain balance and steer.

Mental Health and Cognitive Benefits

  • Reduced Stress and Depression:

    Physical activity releases endorphins and reduces levels of stress hormones like cortisol, helping to alleviate symptoms of depression and anxiety.

  • Increased Mental Focus.
  • Boosted Brain Health.

Practical and Environmental Advantages

  • Cost-Effective Transport.
  • Eco-Friendly.
  • Commuting Efficiency.
